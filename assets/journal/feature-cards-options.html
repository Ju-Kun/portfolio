What's new since the case study

Flip cards with a folded corner. Hover a card: the corner lifts and "Learn more" appears underneath. Click to flip. Feature names and text are placeholders.

Laptop / desktop

Three across

Hover to lift the corner, click anywhere on the card to flip it, click again to flip back. Use the buttons to compare colours for the underside of the corner (the phone preview follows).

Phone

Swipe row

  • Cards sit side by side, so the whole set takes the height of one card instead of three stacked.
  • No swiping (the web app doesn't support it): chevron buttons under the row move one card at a time, and grey out at either end.
  • Each card is a little shorter, and the next card peeks in from the right so it's clear there's more.
  • Phones can't hover, so the corner rests slightly open with "Learn / more" always visible.
  • Dots between the chevrons show which card you're on.
