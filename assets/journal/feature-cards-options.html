What's new since the case study

Two ways to present the new features as cards you can open, and three styles for the button that opens them. Everything is clickable. Feature names and text are placeholders.

Option A

Flip card

The card turns over. Front: label, feature name, one-line teaser. Back: screenshot and two or three sentences.

Option B

Expand in place

A list of feature rows. Clicking one opens it downward with a larger screenshot and room for a full paragraph. Calm, and the easiest to read on a phone.
